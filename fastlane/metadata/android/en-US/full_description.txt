Wazaker is a lightweight Arabic app that helps you remember the Hereafter through selected verses from the Qur'an.

Choose reminder times that suit you, read each verse with its reference and a short reflection, and return to earlier reminders from the archive whenever you wish.

Features:

• Selected verse reminders at times you choose.
• A complete right-to-left Arabic interface.
• An archive of earlier reminders and verses.
• Favorites and a read-later list.
• Personal notes stored on your device.
• Shareable verse images.
• Simple controls for reminders and display preferences.
• No account or internet connection required.

Wazaker is designed to be quiet and lightweight, with no ads, analytics, or tracking.
